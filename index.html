我們做什麼

現有 AI 翻譯與寫作停在句子層。我們先用自研的「語義物理」理論，讓多代理 LLM 管線讀出一部作品的結構：人物引力、劇場規則、伏筆與張力曲線。翻譯與創作再服從這個結構，而不是只服從字面。

Today's AI translation and writing stop at the sentence. We start with structure. Our multi-agent LLM pipeline, grounded in our original Semantic Physics framework, reads a work's character gravity, theatre rules, foreshadowing and tension curves. Translation and creation then obey that structure, not just the words.

逆向分析 NERAS

四軸深讀（人、路、場、底）加對抗審查，產出定位碼可驗證的結構卷宗。

Four-axis deep reading with adversarial audit; a locator-verified structure dossier.

結構保真翻譯

五站工作流。翻譯第一性原則：內在結構 ＞ 意譯 ＞ 音譯。

A five-station workflow under one principle: inner structure > sense > sound.

敘事創造工程

人類定大綱與鎖定項，系統列出每章可能路線並逐層收斂，定稿權在人。

Humans set the outline and locked items; the system enumerates and prunes each chapter's routes; final say stays human.
